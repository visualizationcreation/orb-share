THE SHARED FORMAT

Different subjects. A familiar way to explore.

Submissions must be created using the ORB Skill as described in the field guide, or its published ORB prompt, and preserve the ORB navigation and reading format. Your topic, perspective and visual style can be your own.

  • A clear anchor and connected readings. Give each point a stable identity and a meaningful place in the map.
  • The six-direction compass. UP broadens; DOWN deepens; LEFT/RIGHT move between adjacent facets; FORWARD/BACKWARD follow a labeled thread of implications and precursors. Keep visit history separate.
  • Evidence and honest limits. Link sources to the readings, distinguish interpretation from fact and label unresolved knowledge-floor questions.
  • A usable web artifact. Include a browsable map, a way home, readable mobile layout and a continuation prompt. Offer audio/video only when actual playback is included.

The review checks the finished ORB against this format. A checkbox alone is not format verification. Reading-only ORBs are welcome.

01 / CREATE

03 / OWNER REVIEW

You create. We handle publication.

Informational Dimensions reviews submissions with an AI assistant to inspect the files, check the ORB format, sources and media, and prepare a preview. The owner makes the final decision.

ReceivedReviewedOwner approvedPublished & credited

After approval, we handle the repository, hosting where needed, creator attribution and the Community ORBs listing. If something needs work, we can request an updated submission. Material updates need a new review.

Submissions are reviewed when the owner requests an intake review; there is no continuous AI monitor or guaranteed review time. Submission does not guarantee acceptance. Credit and source provenance stay with the published edition.
